FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Reach out to our 24/7 support team anytime.

What documents do I need to rent a car with Rentor?

A valid driver's license, a major credit card in the primary driver's name, and a government-issued photo ID or passport.

Is there a minimum age to rent a car?

Drivers must be at least 21 years old. A young driver surcharge may apply for drivers aged 21 to 24 on certain vehicle categories.

What is included in the rental price?

Every rental includes standard collision damage cover, third-party liability insurance, 24/7 roadside assistance, and standard mileage allowances.

What is your fuel policy?

We provide full-to-full fuel policies: you receive the vehicle with a full tank and simply return it full to avoid refuel surcharges.

Can I pick up and drop off the car at different locations?

Yes! We offer flexible one-way rentals between all our regional branches and major airport hubs.
